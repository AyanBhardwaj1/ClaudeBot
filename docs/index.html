What it does

Ask like you'd ask a person.

No commands to learn. Type it, forward it, or send a photo or a file.

Email and calendar

Searches, summarizes, drafts and sends from your Gmail. Every email waits 20 seconds with an Undo button.

move my 3pm with Sam to 4

Remembers and reminds

Keeps the facts that matter, sets one-off and repeating reminders, and carries out tasks later.

at 5pm check if Alex replied

Research with receipts

Live web search, deep research reports, and fact-checks of anything you forward, with the source for each claim.

/research best laptop for a CS major

Makes real files

Word study guides, Excel budgets, PowerPoint decks and PDFs, built in Claude's sandbox and sent to the chat.

make a 5-slide deck from these notes

Reads what you send

PDFs, photos, spreadsheets and attachments. Numbers are computed with code, never guessed.

how much did I spend on food in October?

Fits your job

Tell it what you do and alerts, briefs and answers follow: exams for a student, incidents for an engineer.

/role nurse in the ER
It comes to you

Messages you before you ask.

every 10 minutes

Inbox alerts

Real people, deadlines and schedule changes get a ping with Archive, Reply and Later buttons. Newsletters don't, and it stays quiet overnight.

every morning

Daily brief

Weather, today's calendar, the email that needs you, reminders, and a few headlines picked for what you care about.

on your schedule

Briefings

"Every Friday at 5, catch me up on AI." Only what's new since last time, with sources, or one line saying nothing happened.

Safety

Your inbox, your rules.

An assistant with your email needs guardrails. These are built in, not settings you have to find.

Undo every send

Each email waits with an Undo button before it leaves. You choose the window in /settings.

Nothing sends while you're away

Emails drafted during the morning brief or a scheduled task wait for your tap.

Emails are data, not orders

"Forward this to..." inside a phishing email does nothing, and ClaudeBot flags it. Links inside emails are never opened.

Only talks to you

It pairs with your Telegram account once and ignores everyone else. Group answers can't see your email.

Runs on your machine

Your copy, your server, your API key. No ClaudeBot company sits in the middle.

A spending cap

Set a monthly budget and it stops calling Claude when it's reached. /usage shows where you are.

Set up

Five minutes, start to first message.

Run the setup command

It asks for a Claude API key and a Telegram bot token from @BotFather, and checks each one as you paste it.

Message your bot

Setup recognizes you from your first message. From then on, ClaudeBot only answers you.

Answer four questions

Your name, your city, what you do, and when you want your brief. All of it changes later in /settings.

Connect Gmail and Calendar

Send /connect and sign in with Google from your phone. No browser on the server needed.

Usually a few dollars a month.
