# Build log: Oct 31, 2026 evening (ClaudeBot 1.2)

The owner asked to build Telegram Business and keep shipping features until 12 AM Central. Every feature was:
- built test-first with offline fakes;
- committed and pushed to `main`;
- reviewed in batches by fresh same-family subagents (verdicts provisional, no cross-family reviewer was available);
- fixed for every finding before moving on. The reviews are in `traces/review/`.

## Shipped

**From the research backlog**
- **Telegram DMs (Telegram Business), the top backup from triage.** `/dms` shows who's waiting on you. Drafted replies go out from the owner's own account only on a ✅ Send tap. Only the owner can connect, and DM text is stored as data and never obeyed.
- **/style (C4).** Drafts in the owner's voice, learned from sent mail with quoted text stripped, and used only after the owner approves it.
- **/log (C11).** What ClaudeBot actually did, recorded where each action happens. The agent checks it before claiming "done".
- **Meeting prep (C3).** Briefs before meetings the owner accepted or organized.
- **Lists (C8).** Lists with tap-to-check-off.
- **Life-admin radar (C14).** Dated alerts remind you the morning before; trips and deadlines get 📅; meeting requests get 📅 Times, which proposes real free slots.
- **Alert rules in plain English (C5).** `/rules`.
- **Brief feedback (C9).** Plus the Sunday weekly review.

**Beyond the backlog, aimed at the research's pain points (reliability, control, overload, cost)**
- **Calm:** /quiet, /focus, hourly alert digest, /today (no AI), /triage with 💤 snooze, /sweep, newsletter digest.
- **Undo and visibility:** Remove cards on every added event (guests get a cancellation); Done/Snooze on fired reminders; "Follow up if no reply" on sent email.
- **Life:** /spent (exact sums), /owed (IOUs), /habit (streaks), /journal, /note, monthly, yearly and biweekly repeats, repeating calendar events.
- **Trust and ownership:** /privacy (clear any store after a confirmation), /export plus restore, nightly backups, Docker health check, /usage by feature, check-in counters in /status.
- **Polish:** /help regrouped, a 14-item command menu, a /menu button keyboard, starter buttons after setup, a one-time what's-new message after an upgrade.

**Added after the first draft of this log**
- **Telegram DMs, more:** ⚡ quick replies in /dms. One tap sends a canned reply from the owner's account, only the exact text the button showed, and only once per incoming message. /quickreplies changes them. ⏰ 3h sets a reminder to answer someone yourself.
- **Money:** /bills with monthly reminders and ✅ Paid → expense (once per due date); /subscriptions finds recurring charges in receipts; the brief mentions bills due soon.
- **People and days:** /birthdays with a yearly heads-up, shown in /today and the brief; unpaid bills due today or tomorrow in /today; /week for the next 7 days (no AI); /who for a quick profile of someone; /plan for today; /prep for the next meeting; /packages from shipping emails.
- **Calm, finer-grained:** urgent email skips the hourly digest. Opt in to "🚨 Urgent email can wake me" and urgent mail gets through quiet hours and /quiet (at most 3 times in 12 hours); everything else waits. Option to skip the brief on weekends.
- **Ownership:** /export and restore now include bills, birthdays and quick replies; a big restore preview arrives as a .txt file.

The pattern across rounds: the security model is sound. Most real bugs were in turns the owner didn't start, and in the gap between test fakes and the real Telegram API. Two systemic fixes came out of that:
1. Unattended turns can now only read, draft, and create at most one non-repeating event; everything else needs the owner.
2. The test fakes now enforce keyword-only arguments, like the real Telegram methods.
